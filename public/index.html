THE REAL SRI LANKAN RULES

How to play Omi.

01

Four players, two teams

Partners sit opposite. Gold (seats 1 & 3) plays Teal (seats 2 & 4). Deal and play move counterclockwise.

02

Only 32 cards

Use 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, A of all four suits. Ace is highest. Everyone receives four cards first.

03

Pick trumps

The player to the dealer's right selects a trump suit from their first four cards. Everyone receives four more cards (8 total). The trump caller leads.

04

Win the trick

You MUST follow the led suit if you have it. Otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins, or if none was played, the highest card of the led suit. Trick winner leads next.

05

Score tokens

Trump-calling team wins 5–7 tricks: 1 token. Defending team wins 5–7: 2 tokens. All 8 tricks: 3 tokens (Kapothi). A 4–4 draw adds a bonus token to the next decisive hand. First team to 10 wins.

This edition follows classic, automatic-Kapothi rules. Some families play extra announced-Kapothi variations.
